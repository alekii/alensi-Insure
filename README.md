# alensi-Insure

alensi-Insure is a **policy and claims management system** focused on the core operational workflows of insurance administration, rather than attempting to model a full insurance company.

## Core Domain Flow

```text
Customer
   ↓
Policy
   ↓
Coverage
   ↓
Premium
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Claim
   ↓
Assessment
   ↓
Approval / Rejection
   ↓
Settlement
```

## What This System Demonstrates

- Policy lifecycle
- Product and coverage configuration
- Premium calculation
- Policy renewals
- Claims management
- Claims approval workflows
- Payments
- Customer management
- Documents
- Notifications
- Audit trails
